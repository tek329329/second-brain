# Interview queue

Context the brain does not have yet, ordered by how much it blocks useful work. Run `grill-me` on one topic per session (20 to 30 minutes), one question at a time, and the skill saves each answer here as `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`. Remove a topic when its facts have been merged into records or wiki pages.

| Priority | Topic | Why it blocks work | Merge into |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who you are, what you work on, and your priority order | Nothing can be weighed against goals that are not recorded | interview records, `wiki/overview.md` |
| 2 | Your typical week: fixed commitments and free hours | Plans guess at time without it | a `routine` record |
| 3 | Active businesses or projects: what, status, blockers | Area pages cannot exist yet | a state record per project |
